## Selected projects

### [perception3d](https://github.com/aiambo08/perception3d)
Monocular 3D perception: metric depth fusion, object tracking, and time-to-collision / closest-point-of-approach alerts.

- Combines PyTorch, OpenCV and TensorRT with a dual-rate perception pipeline.
- Includes KITTI replay benchmarks, a labeled synthetic demo, tests and CI. Real-camera validation is still pending.

### [armbench](https://github.com/aiambo08/energy-aware-arm-agent)
A UR5e simulation benchmark for LLM-written robot programs, validated skills and energy feedback, built with ROS 2 and Gazebo.

- The repository reports **400 episodes** across five agents and four tasks. Adding validated skills raised success from **62/80 to 78/80** for the LLM agent without energy feedback.
- Energy feedback did **not** demonstrate lower consumption. These results cover one model in simulation, not physical-robot validation.
- Includes episode logs, evaluation reports and an offline replay demo.

### [Physical AI Reliability Agent](https://github.com/aiambo08/nebiusxnvidia_hackathon)
A hackathon prototype for camera-pipeline fault detection, bounded recovery, task-level verification and rollback.

- Includes offline scenarios and tests. Real-hardware validation is pending.

### [AIComply](https://github.com/aiambo08/AIComply)
A local static-analysis CLI for AI code-risk signals and review evidence related to the EU AI Act and GDPR.

- Alpha for supervised local pilots. It is **not legal certification**; no findings does not establish compliance.
